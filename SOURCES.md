# Source and build notes

The additional written evaluation used two raw, uncorrected PDFs from the published student-paper folder. Paper 41 was transcribed from pages 2–4 and paper 7 from pages 2–3. The private exports contain only the assigned written responses. Personal headers, MCQs and Q32 were omitted. Unlocated questions remain missing and one uncertain reading was marked unreadable. The question-mean baseline used 44 other papers after excluding these two and the four earlier development papers. Only aggregate results and content hashes are in the branch.
